About Astro Ankita

Astro Ankita practices Vedic astrology with an emphasis on personal reflection, objective chart analysis, and practical guidance.

Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is an ancient, intricate system that maps planetary positions at the exact moment of birth. Rather than fatalistic predictions, Astro Ankita uses your chart to highlight natural inclinations, favorable periods, and potential areas of personal growth.

Consultation Ethics

  • Complete confidentiality and respect for your personal story.
  • Clear, non-judgmental, and practical explanations.
  • Focus on helping you make informed decisions with self-awareness.
